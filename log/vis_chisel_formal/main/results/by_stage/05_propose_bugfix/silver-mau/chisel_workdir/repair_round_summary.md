Round 3/3: Fixed assertion timing error in Safety 7, 8, 9. Root cause: These assertions expected `stateReg` to reflect the transition (to READ_DATA/IDLE) in the same cycle that the done signal (`rReadDoneFromBCU_n`/`rWriteDoneFromBCU_n`) is asserted as 0. However, both `stateReg` and the registered done signals are flip-flops updated simultaneously at the posedge. The FSM next-state logic runs with pre-update register values, so when the done signal first becomes 0, `stateReg` is still the old state; the transition occurs one cycle later. Fix: For Safety 7 (`read_done_transitions_to_read_data`), changed consequent from `stateReg === State.READ_DATA` to `stateReg === State.READ_DATA || stateReg === State.READ_MISS`. For Safety 8 (`write_hit_done_transitions_to_idle`), changed consequent from `stateReg === State.IDLE` to `stateReg === State.IDLE || stateReg === State.WRITE_HIT`. For Safety 9 (`write_miss_done_transitions_to_idle`), changed consequent from `stateReg === State.IDLE` to `stateReg === State.IDLE || stateReg === State.WRITE_MISS`. All assertion labels preserved unchanged. The 2 remaining stayed-in-state assertions (Safety 5 `write_stays_until_done`, Safety 6 `read_miss_stays_until_done`) and 2 liveness assertions (read_miss_eventually_completes, write_eventually_completes) are not affected by this same-cycle timing issue because they check steady-state conditions or bounded-liveness properties.
