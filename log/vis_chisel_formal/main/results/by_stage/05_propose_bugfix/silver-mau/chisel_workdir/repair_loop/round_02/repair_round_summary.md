Round 2/3: Fixed 7 assertion errors by adding `rRst_n` (registered previous-cycle reset) to antecedent of assertions that check transitions from non-IDLE states. Root cause: All 7 failing assertions used `io.Rst_n` (current-cycle reset) but did not account for reset being asserted during the *previous* cycle, which correctly forces `stateReg` to IDLE via the DUT reset path. The `prevStateReg` captures the state at the start of the cycle, and if reset was active during that cycle (`rRst_n=0`), the FSM overrode the normal transition. Fix: Added `rRst_n` to the antecedent of Safety 5 (write_stays_until_done), Safety 6 (read_miss_stays_until_done), Safety 7 (read_done_transitions_to_read_data), Safety 8 (write_hit_done_transitions_to_idle), Safety 9 (write_miss_done_transitions_to_idle), Liveness 1 (read_miss_eventually_completes), and Liveness 2 (write_eventually_completes). All assertion labels preserved unchanged. This is an assertion_error category with homologous assertions: all 7 share the same `io.Rst_n`-without-`rRst_n` pattern and were repaired identically.
