Round 3/3. Failing properties: cnt_decrements_in_mode1, cnt_stable_in_mode1. Root cause (assertion_error): both mode-1 assertions fail because cnt updates one cycle after io.i is sampled — the DUT's when(io.i && cnt=/=0U) cnt := cnt-1.U takes effect at the next clock edge. For cnt_decrements_in_mode1: at the failure point (time 20), io.i transitions 0→1 at the clock edge, but cnt=1 was computed under io.i=0 in the previous cycle, so expected decrement (cnt == RegNext(cnt)-1 = 0) fails. For cnt_stable_in_mode1: when io.i transitions 1→0 between cycles, cnt still reflects the decrement from the previous io.i=1, violating the stability check. Fix: add !RegNext(io.i, 0.U) guard to both assertions — skip the decrement check when io.i was deasserted in the previous cycle (cnt unchanged), and skip the stability check when io.i was asserted in the previous cycle (cnt may have decremented).
