Round 1/3: Fix controller BUSY-state phantom-ack bug. Failed property: ackA_implies_reqA (also protects homologous ackB_implies_reqB, ackC_implies_reqC). Error category: dut_bug. Root cause: Controller.io.ack was driven directly by ackReg (line 42), but when Client drops req at a clock edge via LFSR rand_choice, the Controller's BUSY handler evaluates io.req at its pre-edge value (1), missing the drop and keeping ackReg high for an extra cycle. This creates a cycle where io.ackA=1 & io.reqA=0. Fix: change io.ack := ackReg to io.ack := ackReg && io.req (combinational AND), so io.ack drops immediately when io.req drops, preserving the ack→req invariant. All assertion labels preserved. Homologous assertions ackB_implies_reqB, ackC_implies_reqC are structurally identical and repaired by the same Controller change.
