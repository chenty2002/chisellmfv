Repair round 1/3: DUT bug fix for `mutual_exclusion_critical_section` (and homologous `mutual_exclusion_L10_active_only`). Root cause: `ticket(selUInt) := false.B` was executed in `Loc.L10` when entering critical section, but the assertion defines BOTH L10 and L11 as the critical section. With ticket cleared at L10, a process in L11 appears ticketless, allowing another process at L7 to bypass the waiting check (`ticket(k) && ...`), enter L10, and violate mutual exclusion. Fix: moved `ticket(selUInt) := false.B` from `Loc.L10` handler (line 124) to `Loc.L11` handler (line 135), inside the `!io.pause` branch where the process actually exits the critical section to `Loc.L1`. This ensures the ticket remains set throughout L10 and L11 (the entire critical section as defined by the assertion), preventing arbitrary entry by other processes. All assertion labels preserved unchanged.
