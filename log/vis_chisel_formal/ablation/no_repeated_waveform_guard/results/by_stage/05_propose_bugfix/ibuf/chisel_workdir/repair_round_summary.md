Repair round 3/3 (setup_error): Added counter-based fairness assumption constraining io.exeReady to fire at least once every 16 cycles (max stall 15). Root cause: the 3 bounded-liveness assertions `slot{0,1,2}_progress_valid_ready_issued_within_16` fail because the formal solver can keep `exeReady` stuck at `00` forever, preventing any instruction from ever issuing — yet the DUT issue logic is correct (issue signals are legitimately gated by exeReady). The fix adds `exeReadyStallCount` (RegInit 0.U(5.W)), incremented when neither exeReady(0) nor exeReady(1) is asserted and reset to 0 on any fire, with `assume(exeReadyStallCount < 16.U)`. This is tight enough for the 16-cycle liveness bound (max stall 15 means a ready instruction waits at most 15 cycles for exeReady, well within the 16-cycle deadline). All three failing properties are addressed by this single environmental fix. Action: new fairness assumption block inserted between opsReady stability assumptions and bounded-liveness assertions.
