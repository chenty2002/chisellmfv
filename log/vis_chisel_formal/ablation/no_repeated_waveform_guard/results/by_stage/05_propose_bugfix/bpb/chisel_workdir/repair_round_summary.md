Round 1: Fix prediction_stable_when_stalled by adding stall_delayed gating. Root cause: prediction register updates on the last unstalled cycle, but assertStableWhen's internal RegNext(prediction) captured the old pre-update value, causing mismatch when stall asserts. Fix: added val stall_delayed = RegNext(io.stall, false.B) and changed when(!io.stall) to when(!io.stall && !stall_delayed) so prediction only updates when stall is low AND was also low the previous cycle. This prevents prediction from changing on the stall-rising edge, allowing RegNext(prediction) to stabilize. Assertion label prediction_stable_when_stalled preserved unchanged.
