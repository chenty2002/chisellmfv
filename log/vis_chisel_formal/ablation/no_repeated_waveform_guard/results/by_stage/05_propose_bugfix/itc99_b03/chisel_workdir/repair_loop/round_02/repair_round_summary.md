Round 2 fix: DUT timing bug in follow-up register update. In `sAnalisReq` (b03.scala line 117-120), the follow-up registers `fu1..fu4` were unconditionally overwritten with `ru1..ru4`. When an input request drops (`ru_i=0`), `fu_i` cleared immediately, but the corresponding grant bit (set in the previous `sAssegna` cycle) persisted through the next `sAssegna` cycle. This created a one-cycle window where `grant(i)=1` and `fu_i=0`, violating all four `grantX_requires_fuY` assertions. Fix: changed `fuX := ruX` to `fuX := ruX || (fuX && grant(Y))`, keeping the follow-up register asserted while its corresponding grant bit is still high. Mapping: fu1↔grant(3), fu2↔grant(2), fu3↔grant(1), fu4↔grant(0). All 4 assertion labels preserved unchanged.
