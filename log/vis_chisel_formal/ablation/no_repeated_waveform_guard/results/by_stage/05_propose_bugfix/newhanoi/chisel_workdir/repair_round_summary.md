Repair round 2/2: assertion_error fix in newHanoi.scala. Root cause: `assertStableWhen(!legal, disc(i), ...)` has incorrect temporal semantics — `legal` is combinational (reflects current-cycle inputs) while `disc(i)` is a register updated based on the *previous* cycle's `legal` evaluation. When a legal move in cycle N updates disc(i) at the cycle N+1 clock edge, and cycle N+1 has illegal inputs, the assertion incorrectly flags the legitimate register update. Fix: introduced `val legalReg = RegNext(legal, false.B)` to snapshot the previous cycle's legality, and changed the assertion condition to `!legalReg`. This aligns the temporal checking: when the *previous* cycle's move was illegal, the disc register should be stable. All 20 `disc_*_stable_when_illegal` assertion labels are preserved unchanged. All 20 structurally identical assertions are repaired in the same way (homologous).
