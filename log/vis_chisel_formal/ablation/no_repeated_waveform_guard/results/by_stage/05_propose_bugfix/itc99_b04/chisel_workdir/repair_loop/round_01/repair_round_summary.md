Round 1: Fixed three FSM transition assertions (trans_sA_to_sB, trans_sB_to_sC, trans_sC_stays_sC) by swapping antecedent/consequent to use forward-looking semantics (RegNext(stato) =/= sX || stato === sY). Original was backward-looking (stato =/= sX || RegNext(stato) === sY) which checked for non-existent reverse transitions. Also fixed four DATA_OUT timing assertions (restart_data_out, average_data_out, ena_no_avg_data_out, default_data_out) by wrapping RHS expressions with RegNext() to snapshot values from the prior cycle, because REG4 and other registers are also updated in the same sC cycle that writes DATA_OUT, causing the comparison against current values to fail.
