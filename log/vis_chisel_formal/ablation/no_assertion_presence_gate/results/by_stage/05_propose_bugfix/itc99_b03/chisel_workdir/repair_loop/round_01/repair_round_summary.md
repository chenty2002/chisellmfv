Repair round 1/3: Added `.otherwise { grant := 0.U }` clause to the `when(fu1||fu2||fu3||fu4)` block in the `sAssegna` state (b03.scala, line ~117). Root cause: when `fu1..fu4` were all 0 (e.g., after a request was deasserted), the grant register retained its stale value, causing `io.GRANT_O(3)=1` while `fu1=0`, violating `assertImplies(io.GRANT_O(3), fu1, "grant_bit3_from_request1")`. The fix clears the grant whenever no follow-up flag is active in sAssegna, eliminating the stale-grant causal path. All 5 failing properties (`grant_bit3_from_request1`, `grant_bit2_from_request2`, `grant_bit1_from_request3`, `grant_bit0_from_request4`, `grant_when_pending`) are addressed by this single change — they all depend on grant clearing when no fu is set. No assertions were modified, deleted, or weakened; all labels are preserved.
