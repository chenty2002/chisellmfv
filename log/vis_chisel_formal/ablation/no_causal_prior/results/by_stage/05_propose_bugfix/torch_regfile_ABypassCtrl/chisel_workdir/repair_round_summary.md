Round 3/3: DUT bugfix targeting 4 failing assertions (A_pipeline_completes_within_8, A_s2e_to_s1m_propagation, B_pipeline_completes_within_8, B_s2e_to_s1m_propagation). Root cause: io.AIgnore_s2e and io.BIgnore_s2e are combinational inputs used in the Phi2 update block (AValid_s1m := AValid_s2e & ~io.AIgnore_s2e). Between the Phi1 posedge (where AValid_s2e is registered and the antecedent conditions are checked) and the Phi2 half-cycle (where AValid_s1m is updated), these signals can transition from 0→1, blocking the s2e→s1m pipeline propagation. The fix adds registered samplers AIgnoreReg_s2e and BIgnoreReg_s2e (latched at the same Phi1 posedge where AValid_s2e is updated), and uses them in the Phi2 assignments instead of the raw inputs. This ensures the ignore decision is sampled consistently with the pipeline stage entry time. Causal link: the assertions sample antecedent at Phi1 (where AIgnore_s2e=0), but the DUT uses the combinational value at Phi2 (where AIgnore_s2e may have changed); registering the Phi1 value breaks this causal mismatch. All assertion labels preserved unchanged. Homologous repair on B-side for the same structural issue.
