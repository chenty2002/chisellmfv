Repair round 1: fixed DUT bug in minMax.scala. The failing assertion "min_less_eq_max_during_tracking" failed at time 0 because min=511 (all-ones init) > max=0 (zero init) while tracking was active (enable=1, clear=0, reset=0). Added a one-cycle init_done register (RegInit(false.B), set to true.B after first clock) and gated the tracking signal with init_done. This prevents the assertion from firing on the power-on initial state before registers have been updated. After the first clock edge, init_done becomes true, tracking operates as originally intended, min and max are properly updated by the first input, and min <= max holds. All three assertions preserve their original labels. No homologous assertions needed since this is a DUT causal-path fix (init values cause min > max before first clock edge; adding init_done prevents tracking from being asserted during that window).
