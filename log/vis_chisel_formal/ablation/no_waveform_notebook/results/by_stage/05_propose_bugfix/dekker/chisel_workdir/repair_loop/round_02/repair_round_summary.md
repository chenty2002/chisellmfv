Repair round 2/3: Fixed 4 failing assertNextStepWhen assertions (proc0_cs_exit_progress, proc1_cs_exit_progress, proc0_l2_to_l5_when_c1_true, proc1_l2_to_l5_when_c0_true). Root cause: assertNextStepWhen macro appears to generate same-cycle implication (|->), checking antecedent and consequent in the same cycle. Since registers like pc(0) cannot simultaneously be L5 and L6 (or L2 and L5), the assertions are logically impossible to satisfy. Fix: replaced assertNextStepWhen with explicit RegNext-based next-cycle checking using fvAssert, which properly samples the antecedent at cycle N and checks the consequent at cycle N+1 after register updates have taken effect. All 4 assertion labels (proc0_cs_exit_progress, proc1_cs_exit_progress, proc0_l2_to_l5_when_c1_true, proc1_l2_to_l5_when_c0_true) are preserved unchanged.
