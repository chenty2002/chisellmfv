Round 2: DUT bugfix addressing both liveness and mutex failures. Two changes in Philosopher class: (1) Added bounded eating counter (MAX_EAT_CYCLES=15) that forces EATING→THINKING transition when counter reaches max even if coin=0 — this fixes the root cause of liveness starvation where a philosopher could hold forks indefinitely; (2) Added priority-based tie-breaker parameter `isEven` — even-indexed philosophers (0,2) additionally check `io.left =/= HUNGRY` when transitioning HUNGRY→EATING, odd-indexed (1,3) have no HUNGRY check — this breaks symmetry preventing two adjacent hungry philosophers from both becoming EATING simultaneously (which caused mutex violations), while avoiding circular deadlock because odd philosophers have no constraint and can always eat first. The causal path for liveness: EATING timeout bounds fork-holding duration → forks become free within 15 cycles → hungry philosopher can eat. The causal path for mutex: left-neighbor hungry check on even philosophers prevents ph0/ph1 or ph2/ph3 simultaneous EATING transitions. All 8 assertion labels preserved unchanged.
