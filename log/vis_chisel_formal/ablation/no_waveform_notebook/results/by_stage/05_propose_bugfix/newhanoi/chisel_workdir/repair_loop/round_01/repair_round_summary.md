Root cause: when(legal) disc(sizeFrom) := io.to unconditionally wrote io.to without validating it's a valid peg (≤2). io.to=3 (invalid) would corrupt disc state, causing all 22 CEXs including disc_0_valid_peg, disc_{1..19}_valid_peg, done_stays_done, and legal_move_to_valid_peg. Fix: changed when(legal) to when(legal && io.to <= 2.U), preventing invalid peg values from ever being written to disc registers. This addresses the causal path — invalid inputs cannot propagate into internal state. All 20 disc_*_valid_peg assertions and legal_move_to_valid_peg assertion labels preserved unchanged.
